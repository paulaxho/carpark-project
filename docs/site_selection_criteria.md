Inclusion Criteria

A site was included if:

* Customer parking was visible in aerial imagery.
* Parking capacity could be reasonably estimated.
* Parking could be attributed to the retail site.
* Parking was predominantly surface parking.

Exclusion Criteria

A site was excluded if:

* No identifiable customer parking existed.
* Parking was heavily obscured.
* Parking consisted primarily of multi-storey structures.
* Capacity could not be estimated reliably.
